      Settings

      Noise suppression On by default. Cleans up a noisy room, but it also trims breaths and quiet trailing words — turn it off if you want to hear your hesitations back exactly as they were.
      Auto gain On by default — boosts quiet audio so your voice and speech pace are picked up reliably. Turn it off if you'd rather hear your natural volume, but a quiet mic may pick up less clearly and transcription can miss speech entirely.
